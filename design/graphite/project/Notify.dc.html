How a card behaves

{{r.k}}{{r.v}}{{r.t}}

Pausing: the timer stops while the pointer is over a card, focus is inside it, its Details are open or an action is running. The thin bar at the bottom shows what is left.

Mute and the per-group switches stop cards, sound and desktop alerts. Activity still records every event. Events from the chat you are looking at stay quiet too.

The stack sits bottom-right, moves bottom-left while the preview pane is open, and runs full width under the header on a phone.
